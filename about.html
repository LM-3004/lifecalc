About LifeCalc

Useful numbers, without the noise.

LifeCalc is a small collection of free, browser-based calculators for everyday decisions. The goal is to make common questions easier to explore with clear inputs, transparent assumptions and no account required.

Private by default

Calculations happen in your browser. LifeCalc does not need your financial details to be sent to a server, and this site does not add advertising or unnecessary tracking.

Built to be clear

Every result is an estimate based on what you enter. We show the basic calculation so you can understand, question and adjust it.
