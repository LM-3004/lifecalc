How it works

A simple estimate, built from your numbers

The calculator converts your income and each expense to a weekly amount. It then compares your current savings with the purchase price and your desired buffer. The estimated timeline uses your weekly income minus weekly expenses, plus any additional weekly saving you enter.

Example

If you have $2,000 saved, want to keep a $1,000 buffer and the purchase costs $1,500, you are $500 short of both goals. A positive weekly surplus can show an estimated number of weeks to close that gap.

Limitations

This is an estimate, not financial advice. It does not account for taxes, irregular costs, interest, price changes, emergencies or changes to your income and expenses. Check your own circumstances before making a decision.

Frequently asked questions

What does “can afford it” mean here?

It means your current savings can cover the purchase while leaving at least the minimum savings buffer you entered. It does not guarantee that the purchase is a good choice.

How are monthly amounts converted?

Monthly amounts are multiplied by 12 and divided by 52. Fortnightly amounts are multiplied by 26 and divided by 52. Yearly amounts are divided by 52.

What if my weekly surplus is zero or negative?

The calculator will say that a timeline cannot be estimated. This means the information entered does not show regular surplus available for this goal.
