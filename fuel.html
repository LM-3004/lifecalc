Understanding fuel costs

A fuel cost calculator estimates how much fuel you may use and what a trip could cost based on distance, fuel consumption and fuel price. This petrol cost calculator converts miles and MPG when needed, then calculates your fuel cost per trip and total cost for multiple trips.

This calculator provides estimates based on the information you enter. Actual fuel consumption and fuel prices can vary depending on driving conditions, vehicle condition, traffic and other factors. This is for general informational purposes only.
