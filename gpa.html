What is a GPA?

GPA is an average of grade points across subjects. Weighting or credits can affect the result when subjects count differently.

What does GPA out of 15 mean?

This calculator uses a 15-point scale from E at 0 through A+ at 15. Weighted results account for each subject's credit or weight.

What does GPA out of 4.0 mean?

The 4.0 value is a simple proportional conversion and may not match an institution's official conversion.

This calculator provides estimates based on the grading scale and information entered. GPA calculation and conversion methods can vary between schools, universities and institutions. The 4.0 result is a proportional estimate and is not an official university GPA conversion.

How to Use the GPA Calculator

Use this GPA calculator by adding each subject, selecting the grade earned, and entering the credit or weight for that subject. When you submit the form, the grade calculator shows your weighted GPA calculator out of 15 result, an unweighted average, and an approximate GPA to 4.0 conversion.

How Does the GPA Calculator Work?

This 15 point GPA calculator multiplies each selected grade point by its credit or weight, adds those weighted values together, and divides by the total weight. That means higher-credit subjects influence the final result more, which is the standard way to calculate GPA for weighted courses.

GPA Calculation Example

Suppose you have three equally weighted subjects with grades A (14), A (14), and A- (13). To calculate GPA, add the grade points and divide by three: (14 + 14 + 13) / 3 = 13.67 approximately, so your GPA calculator out of 15 result is about 13.67/15. Using the site's proportional GPA to 4.0 conversion, 13.67 × 4 / 15 gives approximately 3.64/4.0.

What Is a GPA?

A GPA, or grade point average, is a summary of academic performance across multiple subjects. Schools and universities use it to show how consistently you perform, and a GPA calculator helps turn individual grades into one overall number.

How Does the 15-Point GPA Scale Work?

On this page, the GPA calculator out of 15 uses grade points from E (0) up to A+ (15). Because the scale assigns a numeric value to each letter grade, you can calculate GPA by averaging those points directly or by weighting them according to subject credits.

What Does the GPA Result Mean?

Your result shows your average performance on the 15-point scale based on the grades and weights you entered. The approximate 4.0 value is included as a quick reference, but it is only a proportional conversion from this scale, so official university conversions can differ from what this GPA calculator shows.

Important Note

This grade calculator is designed to provide a helpful estimate of how to calculate GPA on the 15-point scale. The GPA to 4.0 conversion shown here is only an approximate proportional conversion, and official university or institutional conversion methods may use different formulas, cutoffs, or policies.
