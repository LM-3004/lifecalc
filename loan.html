Understanding loan repayments

A loan repayment calculator estimates your regular repayments, total amount repaid and total interest based on the loan amount, interest rate and loan term. This loan interest calculator uses a standard amortizing loan formula and converts the monthly result to your selected frequency.

This calculator provides estimates based on the information you enter. Actual loan repayments, interest charges, fees and loan terms may vary between lenders. This is for general informational purposes only and is not financial advice.
