How to Use the Hourly Wage to Salary Calculator

Our hourly wage calculator helps you understand your annual income based on your hourly rate and work schedule. Here's how to use it:

  • Hourly wage – Enter your gross hourly pay (before tax and deductions).
  • Hours worked per week – Enter your regular weekly working hours.
  • Weeks worked per year – Enter the number of weeks you work annually (typically 52 for a full year, or fewer if you take unpaid leave).
  • Overtime hours per week – If you work overtime, enter the average additional hours per week.
  • Overtime multiplier – Enter the multiplier applied to overtime pay (for example, 1.5 for time-and-a-half).
  • Additional annual income – Include any bonuses, allowances, or other annual income from this role.

Once you've entered your details, click "Calculate salary" to see your gross income breakdown.

How Does the Salary Calculator Work?

This hourly wage calculator estimates your gross income across multiple timeframes by multiplying your hourly rate by hours worked:

  • Weekly gross income – Your hourly wage multiplied by regular hours per week, plus any overtime earnings for that week.
  • Fortnightly gross income – Your weekly gross income multiplied by two.
  • Monthly gross income – Your weekly gross income multiplied by 4.33 (the average number of weeks per month).
  • Annual gross income – Your total earnings for a full year, including regular pay, overtime pay and any additional income.
  • Regular annual income – Earnings from your regular working hours across all weeks worked per year.
  • Overtime income – Additional earnings from overtime hours at your overtime multiplier rate.
  • Daily equivalent income – Your average daily earnings based on your total annual gross income divided by working days.

All calculations are based on the hourly wage, hours, weeks worked, overtime details and additional income you provide. The calculator uses these inputs to estimate gross income before any tax, superannuation or other deductions.

How Much Is My Hourly Wage Per Year?

Calculating your annual income from an hourly wage depends on several factors. Your total yearly earnings are determined by:

  • Your hourly rate – The base amount you earn per hour.
  • Hours worked per week – Your standard weekly schedule.
  • Weeks worked per year – The number of weeks you're paid (accounting for unpaid leave or gaps).
  • Overtime hours and multiplier – Any additional hours at a higher rate.
  • Additional income – Bonuses, commissions, allowances or other payments.

For a simple estimate: multiply your hourly wage by weekly hours, then by 52 weeks. For a more detailed calculation that includes overtime and variations in your schedule, use this hourly rate to annual salary calculator to see exactly how much you make a year.

Important Note

This hourly wage calculator estimates your gross income – the amount before income tax, superannuation contributions, Medicare levy, insurance, or other deductions. Your actual take-home pay may differ significantly depending on:

  • Income tax rates applicable to your income level and location.
  • Superannuation (or pension) contributions.
  • Tax file number (TFN) details and tax withholding.
  • Other personal deductions or circumstances.

This calculator is for informational purposes only and does not provide financial or tax advice. For accurate information about your take-home pay and tax obligations, consult with a tax professional or your employer's payroll department.
