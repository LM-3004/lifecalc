How to Use the Savings Goal Calculator

This savings target calculator helps you understand how long it might take to reach your target amount. Here's how to use it:

  • Enter your current savings: Start by inputting how much money you currently have saved.
  • Set your savings goal: Enter the total amount you want to save. This is your target.
  • Add your regular savings amount: Input how much you can contribute to savings each period. This might be what you put aside weekly, fortnightly, or monthly.
  • Choose your savings frequency: Select whether your contribution is weekly, fortnightly, or monthly. The calculator will convert this to a weekly rate for consistent calculations.
  • Optional: Set a target date: If you want to reach your goal by a specific date, enter it here. The calculator will then show you how much you'd need to save weekly to meet that deadline.
  • Review your results: The calculator will show how long it might take to reach your goal, plus scenarios for saving additional amounts like +$25 or +$50 per week.

How Does the Savings Goal Calculator Work?

Understanding the calculation helps you see how your savings grow over time:

  • Calculate amount remaining: The calculator subtracts your current savings from your goal to determine how much more you need to save.
  • Normalize to weekly savings: Whether you save weekly, fortnightly, or monthly, the calculator converts your regular contribution to a weekly amount. For example, $500 fortnightly becomes approximately $250 per week, and $1,000 monthly becomes approximately $231 per week.
  • Estimate time to goal: The calculator divides the remaining amount by your weekly savings rate to estimate how many weeks it will take to reach your goal.
  • Target date calculation: If you enter a target date, the calculator works backwards. It takes the remaining amount and the number of days until your target date to calculate how much you'd need to save weekly to hit that deadline.
  • Scenario comparison: The calculator automatically shows what would happen if you saved an extra $25, $50, $100, or $200 per week. This helps you see how different savings rates affect your timeline.

Savings Goal Example

Let's walk through a simple example using this savings goal calculator:

  • Current savings: $0
  • Savings goal: $2,000
  • Amount to save: $500 fortnightly

The calculation: $500 fortnightly is approximately $250 per week. You need to save $2,000 (since you currently have $0). Dividing $2,000 by $250 gives you 8 weeks. So, at your current saving rate, it would take about 8 weeks to reach your $2,000 goal.

The calculator would also show what happens if you increased your weekly savings by $25, $50, $100, or $200 — each option would reduce the time needed to reach your target.

What If I Want to Reach My Goal Faster?

The calculator includes alternative scenarios that show how much you could speed up reaching your goal:

  • These scenarios add $25, $50, $100, or $200 to your weekly savings and show the new timeline for each option.
  • For example, if your base calculation shows 8 weeks, saving an extra $50 per week might reduce that to about 6 weeks.
  • These scenarios help you compare different options and see how changes in your savings rate affect when you'll reach your target.

You can use this information to explore what might be realistic and achievable for your situation, but only you can decide what works best for your circumstances.

Important Note

This savings goal calculator provides an estimate based on the information you enter. It assumes your savings contributions and income remain consistent over time and does not account for:

  • Changes to your income or expenses
  • Interest earned on your savings
  • Fees or withdrawals from your savings
  • Inflation or changes in the cost of living
  • Any other unexpected changes in your financial situation

Use this how much do I need to save calculator as a planning tool to help you think through your financial goals. For more detailed financial planning, consider speaking with a financial professional who can take your complete situation into account.
